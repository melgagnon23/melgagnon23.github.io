# Ziggy Heart - Privacy Policy

Ziggy Heart is a private, personal app. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, and is not offered to anyone else.

Google account access is used only by the owner, to connect the owner's own Google accounts (calendar, tasks, email and drive) to the app.

Data from Google stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
